## 1. Brand Essence & Story

### The Mission
CodePath AI guides beginner developers and first-time open-source contributors from intimidation to confident contribution. Unfamiliar codebases often feel like dense labyrinths; CodePath AI lights a clear, bounded path using Python static AST analysis and local AI intelligence.

### The Brand Voice
- **Intelligent & Grounded:** Explanations rely on real code evidence and line numbers, never hallucinations.
- **Developer-Centric:** Built by developers, for developers; clean, modern, terminal-inspired, and distraction-free.
- **Empowering & Trustworthy:** Encouraging to newcomers while rigorously honest about system limitations.

## 2. The Core Symbol Anatomy

The CodePath AI mark is a unified geometric glyph combining three key metaphors into a single continuous silhouette:

```
          [ AI Beacon Node: Contribute with Confidence ]
                       ◉ 
                      / \
                     /   > [ Right Chevron: Open Source Target ]
                    /
  [ Samjho System  ● 
    Waypoint Node ] \
                     \
       <--------------● [ Khojo Code: Entry Bracket Anchor ]
```

1. **CODE — Left Code Bracket (`<`):**
   - Represents entering and exploring the codebase.
   - Styled with clean 46px vector strokes and rounded chamfers in Electric Blue (`#41B4FF`).
2. **PATH — Forward Ascending Route (`/`):**
   - Symbolizes the progression from initial confusion to complete structural understanding.
   - Transitions through an intelligent AST waypoint node (`#41B4FF` with white core spark).
3. **AI — Destination Contribution Beacon (`◉`):**
   - The illuminated milestone at the summit: preparing a confident, well-grounded pull request.
   - Rendered with a glowing violet aura (`#8B5CF6`), orbital dashed resonance ring, and a crisp white core spark.

## 4. Typography

### Primary Wordmark
- **Text:** `CodePath AI`
- **Font Stack:** `system-ui, -apple-system, 'SF Pro Display', 'Inter', 'Segoe UI', Roboto, sans-serif`
- **Weighting:**
  - `CodePath` — Bold / 700 weight, letter spacing `-0.03em`, Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) or Deep Navy (`#0B1020`)
  - `AI` — ExtraBold / 800 weight, letter spacing `-0.02em`, Electric Blue (`#41B4FF`)
- **Capitalization:** Always capitalize exactly as `CodePath AI` (CamelCase "CodePath", uppercase "AI").

### Tagline
- **Text:** `Khojo Code. Samjho System. Contribute with Confidence.`
- **Weight:** Medium / 500 weight, Cool Gray (`#A7B0C0`)

## 6. Sizing & Clear Space Rules

### Clear Space
Maintain a minimum exclusion zone around the mark equal to the height of the letter **"C"** in the wordmark (approx. `0.5 × icon_width`). No text, graphics, or borders should enter this safe zone.

```
       +---------------------------------------------+
       |                  [ Clear Space ]            |
       |     [ICON]     CodePath AI                  |
       |                  [ Clear Space ]            |
       +---------------------------------------------+
```

### Minimum Sizing
- **Horizontal Logo with Wordmark:** Minimum height: `28px` (digital) or `8mm` (print).
- **Standalone Icon:** Minimum dimension: `16×16px`. Below 16px, the mark should not be displayed.

## 7. Brand Dos & Don'ts

### Do:
- Use the transparent primary horizontal logo on dark navy or dark neutral backgrounds.
- Use the light-mode logo on light gray or white surfaces.
- Use the standalone icon for browser favicons, social avatars, and app shortcuts.
- Keep the aspect ratio locked when resizing.

### Don't:
- Do not stretch, skew, or rotate the icon.
- Do not alter the relative sizing between the icon and the wordmark.
- Do not change the color of "AI" to anything other than Electric Blue (or pure white/black in monochrome mode).
- Do not place the colored logo over busy background images without a dark scrim or container badge.
- Do not embed the full tagline inside the compact or standalone icon.
